# Third-party 3D assets

The GLB files in `assets/realistic/nature/` are sourced from the GodotMark nature benchmark repository:
https://github.com/Joshkaki00/godotmark/tree/main/art/nature-benchmark

That project's asset inventory identifies its Poly Haven nature models as CC0 (Public Domain). The upstream inventory is available at:
https://github.com/Joshkaki00/godotmark/blob/main/art/ASSET_INVENTORY.md

Poly Haven assets are released under CC0 1.0. These files are redistributed as game assets; the project does not claim original authorship.


## Additional character, weapon and architecture assets

The Android workflow downloads these CC0 assets into the build workspace before Godot import:

- **Sombrio / Horror Monster Light** — Grab3D, CC0 1.0; 400k triangles, 1K PBR. https://grab3d.com/models/creatures/horror-monster/
- **Man Base Mesh (rigged)** — Grab3D, CC0 1.0; 262,348 triangles, 4K PBR, separate rigged GLB with Walk and Run clips. https://grab3d.com/models/characters/man-base-character/
- **Fantasy Sword Light** — Grab3D, CC0 1.0; 150,004 triangles, 1K PBR. https://grab3d.com/models/weapons/fantasy-sword/
- **Arch bridge stone** — Innerscene original model, CC0; 8.7 MB GLB, detailed dressed sandstone masonry. https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/arch-bridge-stone-719c9452
- **Roman Ruins, Ruin Archway, Broken Column Stump, Ruin Wall Corner and Ruin Wall Segment** — Grab3D Cozy Ancient Ruins Kit, CC0 1.0; modular 2K PBR architecture. https://grab3d.com/kits/cozy-ancient-ruins/

The bridge is a real-world-scale architectural model. The modular Grab3D ruin pieces are stylized PBR assets, used as supporting set dressing rather than represented as photogrammetry. The hero and sword are high-detail PBR assets. All downloaded models are used under their stated CC0 terms.

- **Rocky outcrop** — Innerscene original model, CC0; detailed granite formation with 40 separate blocks, lichen, moss and terrain. https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/rocky-outcrop-9f14ef52
- **Dry stone wall section** — Innerscene original model, CC0; real-scale fieldstone masonry. https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/dry-stone-wall-section-f63adad9
